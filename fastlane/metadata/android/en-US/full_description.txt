Viewer for USB endoscopes and simple UVC cameras connected over OTG.

• live video, starts automatically when the camera is plugged in
• mirror for side-view attachments, rotation, white balance
• photos (also with the camera cable's button) and H.264 video recording
• gallery with up to 10x zoom and frame-by-frame video stepping
• portrait and landscape

No ads, no analytics. Own native layer on libusb and libuvc.
